Returns and refunds

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version: if your order arrives damaged, defective or not what you ordered, we replace it or refund you. We do not take returns for a change of mind, so please check the photos and details first.

How to report a problem

  1. Email hello@pitara.example within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Put your order number in the subject.
  3. Attach clear photos of the problem and the parcel label.

We reply within 1 business day. If confirmed, you choose a replacement (if in stock) or a refund.

What counts as a problem

  • Broken, dead on arrival or visibly damaged.
  • A different product from the one you ordered.
  • A missing item or accessory.

Cancelling

Cancel any time before the order ships. Prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are sent within 7 business days.
  • Prepaid orders go back to the original payment method.
  • Cash on delivery refunds go to a bank account or UPI ID you give us.
